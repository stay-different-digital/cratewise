# Cratewise Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 12 June 2026

Cratewise is a vinyl collection app for collectors and DJs. This policy
describes what data the app handles and where it goes. The short version:
**your data stays on your device, and we collect nothing.**

## Data we collect

None. Cratewise has no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, and no
accounts of its own. The developer receives no data from the app, and no data
is shared with third parties.

## Data stored on your device

- **Your collection and wantlist** (records, notes, condition, custom fields)
  are stored in a local database on your device. They are never uploaded
  anywhere by Cratewise.
- **Your Discogs personal access token**, if you choose to connect a Discogs
  account, is stored only in your device's secure storage (iOS Keychain /
  Android Keystore). It is never logged, never shown in error messages, and
  never sent anywhere except to Discogs itself.

Deleting the app deletes all of this data. You can also remove the Discogs
token at any time via Settings → Disconnect.

## Network connections

Cratewise connects to exactly one external service: the **Discogs API**
(`api.discogs.com`), and only when you use Discogs features (searching the
Discogs database, looking up a scanned barcode, or syncing your collection or
wantlist). These requests are made over HTTPS, on your behalf, authenticated
with your own token.

Discogs is an independent service with its own terms and privacy policy:
<https://www.discogs.com/privacy>. Data you push to Discogs (for example,
adding a record to your Discogs collection) is governed by their policy.

If you never connect a Discogs account, Cratewise makes no network requests
on your behalf other than unauthenticated Discogs database searches you
initiate.

## Camera

The camera is used solely to scan record barcodes, on-device. No photos or
video are stored, and no images ever leave your device. Camera access is
requested only when you open the scanner, and the app works without it.

## Children

Cratewise does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version of Cratewise changes how data is handled (for example,
adding optional crash reporting), this policy will be updated and the
effective date changed before that version ships.
